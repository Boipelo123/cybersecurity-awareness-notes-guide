 CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS GUIDE

 PROJECT DESCRIPTION
 This project serves a purpose of raising awareness and guidance about cybercrime, its types, motivations and effects on individuals, organisation, government and strategic measures that can be adopted to prevent cybercrime.

 PROJECT OBJECTIVES
 - Explain cybercrime
 - Identify types of cybercrime
 - Describe the motivations for cybercrime
 - Explain effects of cybercrime
 - Suggest ways to prevent cybercrime
 - Rise awareness about software vulnerability

PROJECT CONTENTS
-Cybercrime awareness.md: introduction to cybercrime, motivations and their effects
-types of cybercrime.md: examples of cybercrime and prevention tips
- software vulnerability.md: software vulnerability their risks and prevention.

Raising awareness about cybercrime will help individuals and organisations understand it better and take strategic measures to protecting their data and digital systems.
