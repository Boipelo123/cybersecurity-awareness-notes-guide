TYPES OF CYBERCRIMES

PHISHING: These are fraudulent emails or messages pretending to be from legitimate organisations that trick people into revealing their credentials.
IDENTITY THEFT: This is when an attacker steals personal information of the victim to commit other cybercrimes.
RANSOMWARE: Ransomware is malicious software designed to lock and encrypt data then demanding payment for its release.
CYBERBULLYING: Using digital platforms to harass, insult and threaten other people.
ONLINE BANKING FRAUD: These are online deceptive activities used to steal money or gain unauthorised access to financial accounts.
INSIDER ATTACKS: This is when someone within the organisation or an employee who has legal access uses it to commit malicious cybercrimes.
DENIAL OF SERVICE ATTACK (DOS): The attacker floods the system with fake traffic to make it unavailable to real users.
SOCIAL ENGINEERING: This is when attackers manipulate victims into giving them access.

PREVENTION TIPS
- Back up data regularly
- create strong unique passwords
- enable multi-factor authentication
- avoid clicking suspicious links and attachments
- download software from trusted sources

Nowadays, cybercrime is growing rapidly around the world, as new technologies, applications and network emerge. As a result, cybercriminals are taking advantage of system vulnerabilities and for their own benefit. (Regner Sabillion et al, 2016).
