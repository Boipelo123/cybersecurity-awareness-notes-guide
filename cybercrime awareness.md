INTRODUCTION TO CYBERCRIME

Cybercrime refers to any criminal activities committed using computers, digital devices or networks as a target, tool or both.
Examples of cybercrime include ransomware, phishing, identity theft, cyberbullying, online banking fraud and many more.

MOTIVATIONS
- People commit cybercrimes for financial gains by stealing sensitive information for selling, demanding payment for their release.
- revenge and grudges: they target organisations or individuals because they feel wronged and want to settle a score e.g suspended employee
- ego and thrill: some do it for fun and feeling of greatness that comes from beating powerful organisations or individuals.
- espionage: cybercriminals and even nation states steal another county or organisation's valuable data and intellectual property to their advantage.
EFFECTS OF CYBERCRIME ON;
 INDIVIDUALS
- direct financial loss
- identity theft which is used to commit other fraudulant cybercrimes
- psychological impact due to online harrasment and cyberbullying
  
 ORGANISATION
- reputational damage
- financial and revenue loss
- disrupts the organisation's operation
  
 GOVERNMENT
- threatens critical infrastructure
- expensive defensive measure leading to financial loss
- data breach leading to more cybercrime on the nation overhead
  
MITIGATION STRATEGIES
- encrypt and backup data regularly
- have incident response and recovery plan
- protect data by implementing access restriction management
- use Multi-Factor Authentication to create complexity when cybercriminals try to access data
- establish national cyber security defense units
- protect critical infrastructure
